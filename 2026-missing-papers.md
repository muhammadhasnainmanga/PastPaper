# 2026 Missing Past Papers Checklist

Use this checklist for Class 11 (`11`) and Class 12 (`12`). It follows the filename pattern in `pastpaper/papers-data-original`:

`{class}_{subject}_2026_{stream}_{part}.pdf`

Each paper is divided into an **objective** and a **subjective** file. Tick both parts when a paper is complete. The `full` combined PDF is optional and is not counted as a separate paper here.

## Stream and subject rules used

- English, Islamiyat (Class 11), Mathematics, Physics, Urdu, and Pakistan Studies (Class 12): one paper for `pre-medical-and-pre-engineering` and one for `science-general`.
- Chemistry: one shared paper for `pre-medical-and-pre-engineering` per class.
- Biology-related papers: Botany and Zoology for `pre-medical-and-pre-engineering` per class.
- Computer Science: `science-general` only (Class 12 uses the stream-specific filename convention present in the data).
